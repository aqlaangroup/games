# Qahwet Sitti (قهوة ستّي): the game and the plan

Source research: `research/reports/Qahwet Sitti concept deep research.md`.
Playable prototype: `prototype/index.html` (open it in a browser).

## 1. What the game is

**One line:** two cousins, one from Amman and one from Riyadh, inherit their grandmother's café and have to run it together.

- **Type:** merge-2 puzzle with a story and a café makeover. This type is proven: Gossip Harbor (79% female players) is in Saudi Arabia's top 10 by revenue.
- **Player:** Arab women aged 25–45. Saudi Arabia and the UAE are the paying markets. Jordan and Egypt are the test and volume markets.
- **Platform:** iOS and Android, portrait, sessions of 5–10 minutes.

### What changed from the first idea, and why

| First idea | New version | Why (from the research) |
|---|---|---|
| One Jordanian heroine | **Two heroines:** Salma (Amman) and Lana (Riyadh), cousins who co-own the café | Saudi Arabia is about 59% of the region's game spending. Saudi players need to see themselves in season 1, not season 2. |
| Jordanian dialect everywhere | Neutral Arabic menus. Salma speaks urban Amman dialect, Lana speaks Najdi. Tap any local word to see its meaning. | "Poor dialect choices" is the top complaint about Arabic games. Gulf players may find Jordanian dialect harder. |
| Paid items only | **Hybrid:** in-app purchases for the Gulf, plus optional rewarded ads for Jordan and Egypt | Spending per player: about $85 in the UAE, $55 in Saudi Arabia, $3.40 in Egypt. |
| Ramadan as the main sales time | Buy players 2–4 weeks before Ramadan. Ramadan = 30-night story event after iftar. Eid = gifts and bundles. | Data on Ramadan game spending is mixed. Game sessions are longest in Ramadan. |
| AI voice | Human voice actor for Sitti and the heroines. AI only for item variations, backgrounds and event art. | Arabic dialect AI voice drifts into the wrong dialect. Players react badly to AI voices. |
| Many events from day one | 5 reusable event templates (race, album, 28-day pass, streak, frenzy), about 20 events a month at launch | Gossip Harbor started at about 20 a month and reuses 64 event types. |

### The loop

1. **Collect:** tap Teta's coffee tin, the semolina sack or the tea box. Each tap uses 1 energy.
2. **Merge:** two identical items make a better one (beans → qahwa, semolina → knafeh, mint → tea tray).
3. **Serve:** neighbors order items and pay coins.
4. **Restore:** spend coins to fix the café, choosing a style (Old Levantine, Modern Ammani, Gulf majlis).
5. **Story:** each restoration unlocks the next scene. Choices change some lines.

### Rules that protect the player

- Simple item chains (4–5 steps), and the board always has free space to work with.
- No paid random boxes. Every purchase shows exactly what is inside.
- Solo first. Social features are gifting and café visits. No open chat with strangers.
- Content suitable for a 12+ rating: a family-centred, marriage-minded romance, modest outfits, no alcohol or casino imagery.
- Age check at first launch. No ad tracking of minors.

## 2. Score

| Area | Research score | New version | What changed it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fit with the audience | 9.0 | 9.3 | Saudi co-heroine, solo-first design, Saudi-first marketing |
| Proven mechanics | 8.5 | 8.7 | Simple chains and the free-board rule; working prototype |
| Cultural hook | 8.5 | 9.2 | Two cities, two dialects, tap-to-explain words |
| Money potential | 6.5 | 7.5 | Hybrid ads and purchases; Gulf-first bundles and a 28-day pass |
| Standing out | 8.0 | 8.8 | No direct competitor; Arabic-first; two-cousin drama |
| Cost and difficulty | 6.0 | 7.3 | Event templates, AI for variants only, one café per season |
| **Average** | **7.8** | **8.5** | |

**About the 9:** on paper the honest score is **8.5**. The gap to 9+ is in money and cost, and only real player data can close it. The plan below is built so the game **only moves forward when it measures at 9 or higher**. If it doesn't, you stop early and cheaply.

## 3. The plan: go only at 9+

| Stage | What you do | Must reach to continue (9+ level) | Rough cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Rules | Check 12+ content, UAE child-safety law, creator permits | Written answers | Small |
| 1. Words | 30 story lines tested with Saudi, Jordanian and Egyptian women | 90%+ understand without help | Small |
| 2. Ad test | Story ads vs generic merge ads in Saudi Arabia, Jordan and Egypt | Story ads win by 20%+ | $2–5K per country |
| 3. Vertical slice | Grow this prototype: art, sound, voice, chapter 1, two events | Testers want to keep playing | Jordan's KAFD grant, JD 5–10K |
| 4. Retention test | Android soft launch in Jordan or Egypt, 900+ installs per group | Day 1 40%+, day 7 15%+, day 30 7%+, crashes under 1% | about $1–4K in ads |
| 5. Money test | iOS and Android in Saudi Arabia | 3%+ of players pay; ads pay back in under 12 months | about $9–18K in ads |
| 6. Funding | Pitch with real numbers to Tamatem, Merak, Impact46 | A deal | Merak rounds: $480K–1.5M |
| 7. Launch | Scale ads 2–4 weeks before Ramadan 2028 | Cost per install rises under 50% when spend triples | From stage 5 |

**Stop rules:** day-1 retention under 25%, or story ads not beating generic ads, means stop or change the idea before spending more.

## 4. Team

- **Prototype to soft launch:** 4–6 people (2 developers, 1 artist, 1 writer for Amman dialect plus 1 Saudi writer for Lana, 1 producer). Use freelancers where possible.
- **Live game:** 8–12 people to run about 20 events a month.

## 5. What the prototype already shows

- A merge board with three item chains (coffee, sweets, tea) and generators.
- Customer orders with coins and levels.
- Energy that refills over time, plus a rewarded-ad button (simulated).
- Six café restorations with style choices that change the café picture.
- Season 1 chapter 1 story with characters, choices and tap-to-explain words.
- Progress saved on the device.

Not included yet: real art, sound, voice, events, shop and payments, analytics.
