Six pillars

What makes it worth playing

A living 3D café

Light follows the real time of day, rain falls in winter, and customers walk in, sit, chat and leave.

Playable memories

Every chapter opens a scene from Amman in 1974 that you play with your hands. 8 game types, never the same twice.

A family mystery

Two sisters who stopped speaking 20 years ago, letters hidden in the café, and a coffee-chain owner who wants the building.

Neighbours with lives

Abu Fadi's lost love, Tala's final school exams, a young widow's catering business. 36 small stories in season 1.

Self-expression

Dress Salma for each chapter's occasion and share your café as a postcard on Snapchat, Instagram or WhatsApp.

Warm social play

Invite friends to help with big orders like weddings. Visit their cafés and leave a gift. No chat with strangers.

What stays: the merge kitchen, energy, orders and levels from the first design. It's proven with this audience (Gossip Harbor: 79% female, top-10 grossing in Saudi Arabia), and it pays for everything else.
How it plays

A session, 2 to 10 minutes

  1. OpenThe café shows the real time of day, with regulars at their tables.
  2. MergeA few minutes in the kitchen to make what customers want.
  3. ServeDrag the cup to a customer and watch them react.
  4. DecorateSpend coins on a task or a piece and place it yourself.
  5. StoryA scene plays. The chapter's last task opens a 1974 memory.
  6. ShareDress Salma, snap a postcard, visit a friend.

Then the player comes back later, when energy has refilled and a new regular is due at the café.

Roadmap

Five stages, each with a gate

We only spend on the next stage when the current one proves itself.

Months 1–2

Pre-production

Art bible, a 3D style test in the engine, and the season 1 story bible.

Gate: the 3D café runs at 30+ fps on a 3 GB Android
Months 3–5

Vertical slice

One decoratable café room, the merge kitchen, Abu Fadi, chapter 1, the pour memory and the letter.

Gate: 70%+ of 30–50 women in Amman and Riyadh want to keep playing
Months 6–9

Alpha

Chapters 1–5, shop, rewarded ads, analytics and the shared .NET platform.

Gate: crashes under 1%
Months 10–11

Beta

The full season with hosting, postcards, the Sitti wall and Arabic voice for key moments.

Gate: testers finish the season
Months 12–14

Soft launch

Jordan and Egypt first, then Saudi Arabia.

Gate: 40% return on day 1 · 3%+ pay in Saudi Arabia
Months 15–16

Launch

Saudi Arabia, the UAE and the wider region, with season 2 in production.
