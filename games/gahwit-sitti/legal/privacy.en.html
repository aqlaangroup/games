Gahwit Sitti: privacy policy

Last updated: [publication date]

Who we are

[Company legal name], [address] ("we") makes the game Gahwit Sitti (قهوة ستّي) and runs its servers. Contact us at [support email], or from Help in the game.

What we collect

  • An account id we create for you, and a secret that keeps you signed in on your phone.
  • If you link Apple or Google: the id Apple or Google gives us for you. We never see your password.
  • Your birth year (only the year), the game's language, and your country if your phone shares it.
  • Your game progress, saved on your phone and, when you're online, on our servers.
  • Purchases: the store's transaction id, the product, its price and date. Apple or Google handle your payment; we never see your card.
  • How you play: events such as finishing a task or a memory, with your phone's model and the game's version, and error reports when something goes wrong.
  • Messages you send from Help, and the email address if you choose to give one.
  • Your network address, only for a moment, to stop abuse. We don't store it.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or advertising id. The game has no open chat.

Why we use it

  • To run the game and keep your progress safe in the cloud.
  • To deliver what you buy, restore it, and handle refunds.
  • To keep rewards fair: our servers check them before giving gems.
  • To improve the game, using play events and error reports.
  • To answer you when you write to us.
  • To follow the law, including age rules for children.

Ads

The game shows no ads today. If we add rewarded ads, they will always be your choice, we will name the ad partner here first, and players under 13 or whose age we don't know will get no personalised ads.

Children

The game asks for your birth year when it first starts. Players under 13 get no personalised ads and no social features until a parent agrees. We collect no more than the game needs. A parent can ask us to delete a child's data at [support email].

Who we share it with

  • Our hosting provider, [hosting provider], which stores our servers' data for us.
  • Apple and Google, to check purchases and sign-ins.
  • Nobody else, except when the law requires it. We never sell your data.

Where it is kept

On servers in [region], protected by encryption in transit and access controls. Every time our staff look at a player's account, it is recorded.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. When you do (Settings, Delete my account), your account ends at once and we delete its data within 30 days. We keep records of purchases for accounting and tax, without your account linked to them. Play events may be kept longer only in a form that no longer identifies you.

Your rights

You can see, correct or delete your data, and withdraw consent, by writing to [support email] or deleting your account in the game. These rights follow Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, the UAE's Personal Data Protection Law and Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law 151 of 2020). You may also complain to your country's data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above, and tell you in the game when the change matters.
