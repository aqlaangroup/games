# 20 · Social: postcards, friends, visits, hosting and the Sitti wall

**Ring 2 (postcards) · Ring 3 (everything else) · Size L · Spec:** `SYSTEMS_B.md` 7, 8; `GAME_SYSTEMS.md` 14; `COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 2, 14; `balance.json` `social`, `levelB.hosting`, `levelB.sittiWall`

## Where it stands

- Nothing is built, in the game or on the server.

## What's missing

**Postcards (ring 2)**
- One tap makes a picture of your café with Salma in her outfit, a soft frame and the game's name.
- Share it through the phone's share sheet to Snapchat, Instagram or WhatsApp, with a download link. The link needs the store app ids (21).
- A postcard maker screen.
- Unlocks at level 10.

**Friends and gifts (ring 3)**
- Friend codes and a friend list. No open chat.
- Send 5 energy gifts a day.
- On the server: friends, codes and gifts, each table with a `GameId`.

**Visits (ring 3)**
- Walk around a friend's café in 3D, and leave one gift a day.
- Needs a read-only copy of their café from their save (`cafe.placed`), and the `Visit` scene.

**Hosting (ring 3)**
- **The big order:** twice a week, lasting 48 hours, needing 30 items.
- **Helpers:** invite up to 3 friends, by code or from the friend list. Every item a helper gives counts.
- **Playing alone works:** neighbour bots help, for smaller rewards.
- **Rewards:**
  - the host: gems and a decor piece
  - each helper: gems and +10 friendship
  - everyone, if the order is finished: a shared postcard
  - The gem amounts are missing (02).
- **Messages:** 12 preset messages, such as «قهوتك بتجنّن». Only one is written.
- **On the server:** hosting orders, contributions, bots and rewards. Rewards are granted on the server.
- **Push:** "friend needs help" (19).

**Sitti wall (ring 3)**
- Pin your own grandmother's saying or recipe, up to 140 characters.
- **Moderation before anyone sees a post:** automatic first, then a person.
  - The automatic filter must handle Arabic.
  - The person works from a queue in the admin panel (22).
- Posts are shown without names. Players can like them but not reply.
- **Missing from the spec:** Apple's rules for user content also ask for a way to report a post and block a user, and for published contact details. Add a report button and a block action.

**Age rules**
- Under 13, or age unknown: no social features until a parent agrees (04).

## Done when

- A postcard can be made and shared from a phone (ring 2).
- Two test accounts can add each other by code, send gifts, visit, and finish a hosting order together.
- A Sitti wall post appears only after a person approves it, and other players can report it.
