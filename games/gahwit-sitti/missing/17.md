# 17 · Purchases, shop and rewarded ads

**Ring 2 · Size L · Spec:** `GAME_SYSTEMS.md` 11, 12; `SYSTEMS_B.md` 9; `COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 1, 10, 11, 14; `game.json`; `/platform/README.md`

## Where it stands

**In the game**
- `game.json` lists 10 products.
- Unity IAP (`com.unity.purchasing`) isn't installed. There's no ads SDK and no shop screen.
- `_Game/Core/Wallet.cs` shows the platform's gem balance, but nothing ever sets it.

**On the server**
- `/purchases/verify` exists on PostgreSQL. It takes the player from the access token, and games register from `game.json`.
- But the checker still refuses every receipt (`NotConfiguredVerifier`).
- The Apple and Google webhooks are empty.

## What's missing

**In the game**
- **Unity IAP** with the products from `game.json`, showing each country's store price.
- **The purchase flow:**
  1. The player buys.
  2. The store sends a receipt.
  3. The platform verifies it.
  4. The game shows the new balance.
  5. Only then does the game finish the transaction, so an unconfirmed purchase is retried on the next launch (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 10).
- **Grants that aren't gems.** Energy, a generator copy, a decor set and an outfit live in the save, not on the server.
  - **Decide** how the game applies them, for example from the platform's reply, once per transaction id.
  - Write the decision down.
- **Restore purchases** (an Apple requirement), for the Eid bundle and the pass.
- **Screens:** shop, starter pack offer, bundle offer, purchase result (`screens/Shop`, `screens/Starter`).
- **Shop rules:**
  - The starter pack offer appears from level 3.
  - No shop and no ads before level 3.
  - Every bundle lists its exact contents, and nothing is a paid random item.

**On the server** (with 03)
- **Apple:** verify through the App Store Server API, and handle App Store Server Notifications v2 for refunds and renewals.
- **Google:**
  - Verify through the Play Developer API.
  - Handle real-time developer notifications.
  - Acknowledge every purchase, or Google refunds it after 3 days.
- **Rules:**
  - A store transaction id grants once.
  - `oncePerPlayer` applies to the starter pack.
- **Refunds:** take back what was granted. Decide what happens when the gems are already spent.

**The season pass**
- `game.json` makes `gs.pass` a subscription with `periodDays: 28`.
- Apple's auto-renewing subscriptions come in 1 week, 1, 2, 3 or 6 months, or 1 year. There's no 28-day period.
- **Decide:** a monthly subscription, or a one-off purchase for each 28-day season.

**Rewarded ads**
- **Choose:** LevelPlay or AppLovin MAX (`GAME_SYSTEMS.md` 16).
- **Placements and daily caps:**
  - +25 energy, 5 a day
  - double an order's coins, 3 a day
  - pop a bubble, 2 a day
- Count the caps in `daily.adsWatched`.
- No interstitial ads.
- No personalised ads for under-13s or players whose age is unknown (04).
- Import ad revenue reports into the admin panel (22).

**Gem spending** (all through the platform)
- Energy refills, bubbles, storage slots, premium decor and skipping a memory retry.

**Season products not in `game.json` yet** (`SYSTEMS_B.md` 9)
- 4 premium decor sets at about $4.99.
- 4 outfit bundles at about $2.99–$4.99.

**Store setup**
- Create the same SKUs in App Store Connect and Google Play (21, 23).

## Done when

- A sandbox purchase on each store grants once, even when the receipt is sent twice.
- A sandbox refund takes the purchase back.
- Restore brings back the Eid bundle on a new install.
- A rewarded ad gives +25 energy, up to 5 times a day.
