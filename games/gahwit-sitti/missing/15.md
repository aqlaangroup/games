**Ring 1 (accessibility rules, basic settings) · Ring 2 (full settings) · Size M · Spec:** `COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 4, 5, 8, 12; `/CLAUDE.md` (accessibility)

## Where it stands

- The interface is built in code (`_Game/UI/UiKit.cs`) on a 1080 × 1920 canvas.
- A merge makes the new item pop. Other moments show a toast.
- `_Game/UI/SettingsView.cs` (added 2026-10-31) has:
  - the cloud save line, language and legal rows
  - switches for sound, music, notifications, glossary and voice
  - account, help and delete rows
- Nothing plays sound yet, so the sound and music switches have no effect (14).

## Touch targets under 44 pt

`/CLAUDE.md` asks for 44 pt or more. The canvas scales with "Expand", so on a 390 pt wide iPhone one design unit is about 0.36 pt. 44 pt is about 122 units.

## Settings still missing (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 4, `screens/Settings`)

- **Haptics switch.** The save has the field, but the screen doesn't show it.
- **Notifications, one switch per type** (19). The save has a single `notifications` switch, so this needs a schema change.
- **English layout.** English text loads, but the HUD and screens are laid out right to left only. English should flip to left to right.
- **Neutral-Arabic subtitles** for dialogue (11).
- **Text size:** normal or large (about 1.2×).
- **Graphics quality:** auto, low or high (23).
- **Reduced motion.**
- **Memory easy assist** (12). The save has no field for it yet.
- **Restore purchases** (17).

## Accessibility (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 5)

- **Large text mode,** with nothing smaller than 12 pt.
- **Reduced motion** that follows the phone's own setting.
- **Ready orders.** Today they change colour and their button text, which is fine for colour-blind players. The spec also asks for a tick.
- **Dialogue never advances on its own.** This is already true.
