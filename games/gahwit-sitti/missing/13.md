## Where it stands

- **No art assets exist.** `art/` holds only the prompt pack.
- **The game draws everything in code** with shapes and colours: the café, items, icons and interface (`_Game/Cafe/CafeRoom.cs`, `_Game/UI/ItemLook.cs`, `_Game/UI/UiSprites.cs`).
- **`art/licences/` doesn't exist** (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 14).
- **Images saved in `art/` would not go through Git LFS.** The LFS rules are in `unity/.gitattributes` and only cover `unity/` (23).
- **The designs in `screens/` use the earlier look.** `COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 12 says they need updating, and many level B screens have no design yet (below).

**Pipeline**
- **Import rules:** textures up to 2K, shared atlases, compression per platform, and LODs.
- **Addressables groups:** decide what ships in the app and what downloads later (23).
- **Folders:** `Assets/Art/`, by area (`unity/README.md`).
- **Licences:** every asset-store pack, font and AI tool's terms go in `art/licences/`.
- **AI use:** follow the rule in `PLAN.md` 6. Characters, key art and main voices are made by people. AI is for variations and drafts.
