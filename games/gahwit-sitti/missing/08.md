# 08 · Customers and regulars

**Ring 1 (walk-ins, Abu Fadi to friendship level 3) · Ring 2 (6 regulars) · Ring 3 (12 regulars) · Size M · Backlog 13, 14 · Spec:** `SYSTEMS_B.md` 3; `GAME_SYSTEMS.md` 6; `balance.json` `levelB.walkIns` and `levelB.regulars`

## Where it stands

- Customers are only names on order cards. Nobody walks into the café.
- Abu Fadi places orders and speaks in story scenes, but has no friendship level.
- The save keeps a `regulars` section but doesn't use it.
- Walk-in names come from `chapters.json` `walkIns` (4 ids).

## What's missing

**Walk-ins (backlog 13)**
- **The path:**
  1. Walk in the door to a free table and sit.
  2. Show the wanted item over their head.
  3. Get served from the board, sip, and leave a 2–10 coin tip.
  4. Leave.
- They leave after 10 minutes if no one serves them, with no penalty.
- 2–6 at a time, depending on cosiness (07).
- **Undecided:** how walk-in wishes relate to the order cards in the kitchen. Are they the same orders shown in two places, or a separate kind? The spec doesn't say. Decide before building.
- The rules live in a plain C# class, such as `CustomerFlow` driven by trusted time, with tests. The view only moves the figures.
- Pool the character objects (`/CLAUDE.md`, performance).

**Regulars (backlog 14)**
- **Friendship** goes from level 1 to 10. A favourite item gives +3 points and any other item +1. Each level needs 10 more points than the last.
- **Story scenes** at levels 3, 6 and 10, and a unique decor piece at level 10.
- **Visits** at set times of day (`levelB.regulars.schedule`).
- **A short line** when they order (`GAME_SYSTEMS.md` 6).
- **Favourite items** aren't defined for anyone yet. Add them to the content, not to `balance.json`.
- **Screens:** a regular's profile and the cast list (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 12).
- **Save:** the `regulars` section is `{ points, level, storiesSeen }` per regular.
- **Unlock:** friendship levels unlock at player level 6 (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 2). Abu Fadi reaches level 3 in ring 1, so decide whether he's an early exception.

**Characters on screen**
- Placeholder figures now, real art later (13).
- One shared animation set: walk, sit, sip, idle, talk, laugh, wave, react.
- Waypoints or a NavMesh. `com.unity.ai.navigation` is already installed.

**Content by ring**
- **Ring 1:** Abu Fadi up to level 3, with his first scene.
- **Ring 2:** 6 regulars.
- **Ring 3:** all 12, with 36 scenes in total (11).

## Done when

- Abu Fadi walks in, sits, orders, gets served, sips and reacts (backlog 13).
- Reaching friendship level 3 with him plays his first story scene (backlog 14).
- Walk-ins come and go with cosiness, and leave after 10 minutes unserved.
