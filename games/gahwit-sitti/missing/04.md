# 04 · Connecting the game to the platform

**Ring 1 · Size S–M · Spec:** `COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 1 (step 1), 4, 9, 10, 11, 14; `screens/AgeGate`, `screens/Settings`

Updated on 2026-10-31 after commit `9e59fbd` (age gate and cloud choice).

## Where it stands

**Built** (`_Game/Boot/AccountService.cs`, `_Game/UI/AgeGateView.cs`, `CloudChoiceView.cs`, `SettingsView.cs`):
- **Boot:**
  1. Signs in, as a guest the first time.
  2. Takes the server's time.
  3. Compares saves: upload, download, or ask "this phone or the other one".
- **Age gate:** shown on first launch, and for saves from before schema v2.
- **Linking Apple:**
  - links this account to Apple
  - moves the phone to the café that Apple id already has
- **Settings:** support messages and account deletion.
- **Offline:** the game keeps working, and the cloud catches up later.

## What's missing

**Gems**
- Nothing calls `Wallet.ApplyPlatformBalance`, so gems still show 0.
- Level-up and chapter gems are never claimed. That needs the rewards route (03), plus a queue of rewards earned while offline.

**Android**
- **The session token** is kept in `PlayerPrefs` (`_Game/Platform/DeviceSecrets.cs`). Use a store backed by the Android keystore, through a small Kotlin plugin.
- **Google sign-in on the phone:**
  - a Credential Manager plugin
  - the client ids in `game.json` (`stores.google.signInClientIds`)
  - the link and sign-in buttons on Android

**Age rules in the game** (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 14)
- The age band is saved, but nothing acts on it yet. Under 13, or age unknown, needs:
  - no personalised ads (17)
  - no social features (20) until a parent agrees
- **Undecided:** how a parent agrees. Options include a store family feature, an email to the parent, or keeping social off. Get legal advice (21).
- Egypt's under-15 rules are marked "pending legal review" in `_Game/Core/AgeRules.cs`.

**Offline and errors** (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 10)
- The gentle «بدنا إنترنت لهاد» message on features that need a connection.
- A small banner when the server is down, shown only when the player opens an online feature.
- An "update required" screen, once the platform sends a minimum version (03).

**Settings rows still missing** (see 15)
- Restore purchases (17).
- Privacy and terms open placeholder addresses until the pages exist (21).

**Housekeeping**
- `DEVLOG.md` has no entry for the platform client, the age gate or the cloud choice (01).

## Done when

- Gems in the HUD match the platform's wallet, and a level-up claims its gems once.
- An Android phone keeps its session in the keystore and can link Google.
- An under-13 answer turns off social features and personalised ads.
- Online features show the gentle offline message when there's no connection.
