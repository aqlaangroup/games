# Gahwit Sitti: rating and what's missing

Reviewed on 2026-10-31. The review covered:
- `BUILD_GUIDE.md`, `PLAN.md` and `CONCEPT.md`
- `design/` and `game.json`
- the Unity project
- the shared `/platform` and `/unity-shared`

The game folder's first commit was on 2026-10-30.

Paths in these files are relative to `games/gahwit-sitti/`. `_Game/` means `unity/Assets/_Game/`.

## Rating

| Area | Score | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Concept | 8.5 / 10 | A clear hook (1974 memories, a family mystery, an Amman café) for a defined audience. Backed by research. |
| Design docs | 8 / 10 | Short and buildable, with a coverage map. Some numbers disagree between files (02). The story bible doesn't exist yet. |
| Economy and balance | 8 / 10 | One numbers file and a pacing simulator, with every chapter on target. Some spec numbers are not in `balance.json`. The simulator is not a C# test yet. |
| Code quality (what exists) | 8 / 10 | Rules are plain C#, with 49 EditMode tests. The random numbers are seeded, time is trusted, saves migrate, and gems belong to the server. Weak spots: small touch targets, a few hard-coded numbers, and a client that doesn't match the server. |
| Ring 1 progress | 4.5 / 10 | 11 of 24 backlog tasks done, 5 partly done, 8 not started. The heavy tasks remain: decor, customers, the tutorial. |
| Story content | 1.5 / 10 | Chapter 1 only: 6 of 60 tasks, 1 of 10 memories, 1 letter. 5 of chapter 1's 6 task scenes are `[draft]`. |
| Art, audio, video | 0.5 / 10 | Everything on screen is code-made shapes. There's a prompt pack but no assets and no sound. |
| Platform server | 2 / 10 | Never compiled. Data lives in memory. No sign-in and no saves. The game calls 8 routes the server doesn't have. |
| Money, ads, analytics, live ops | 0.5 / 10 | Nothing installed or built. |
| Launch readiness | 0.5 / 10 | Legal pages, store pages and support are placeholders. |

**Overall**
- **As a plan: 8 / 10.** It's thorough, consistent in most places, and honest about budget and risk.
- **As a game today: 3 / 10.** Chapter 1 works end to end as a prototype: the merge kitchen, café tasks with style choices, the pour memory and the letter.
- **Progress (estimates):**
  - Ring 1 code backlog: about 55%, counting S tasks as 1 and M tasks as 3.
  - Ring 1 as a whole, with art, cloud save and the tester gate: about 35%.
  - Ring 2 (soft-launch MVP): under 10%.
  - Ring 3: none.

For two days of work, that's fast progress on a solid base.

## What's strong

- **Rules are separate from views.** `_Game/Merge/Game.cs` has no Unity objects, and the views only draw it.
- **49 EditMode tests:** Merge 14, Platform 15, Core 10, Save 5, Memories 5.
- **One source for numbers:** `design/balance.json`, copied into the game by `Editor/BalanceSync.cs`.
- **Trusted time:** server time plus the phone's uptime counter, with a test that changing the clock adds no energy.
- **Saves follow `design/save_schema.json`,** with tested migrations (v0 → v1 → v2).
- **Arabic text** comes from Unity Localization and renders with RTLTMPro. A test checks every key has both languages.
- **`DEVLOG.md`** records decisions and known gaps.

## Biggest risks

1. **No art bible, style test or art.** `PLAN.md` makes the style test a stage 0 gate, and art is the stated bottleneck (13).
2. **The platform is a skeleton,** and the game client was written against routes that don't exist (03, 04).
3. **No Android build and no test on a 3 GB phone.** That's the other stage 0 gate (23).
4. **No story bible.** Chapters 2–10 can't be written well without it (11).
5. **Spec conflicts** on storage slots, unlock levels, product ids and style ids (02).
