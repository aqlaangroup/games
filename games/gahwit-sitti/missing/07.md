# 07 · The 3D café: decor, cosiness, camera, time and weather

**Ring 1 (15 decor pieces, cosiness, camera, performance) · Ring 3 (rooftop) · Size M–L · Backlog 11, 12 · Spec:** `SYSTEMS_B.md` 2, 9; `COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 2, 12, 13

## Where it stands

- `_Game/Cafe/CafeRoom.cs` builds the room from simple shapes, ported from `cafe-3d/index.html`.
- The six chapter 1 tasks each change one part of the room: sign, floor, chairs, dallahs, lights and counter. The sign and chairs take a style.
- A 1974 look with faded colours and film grain is used for memories.
- The light follows three times of day, picked once at launch.
- `_Game/Cafe/CafeCamera.cs` orbits about 120° and zooms with a pinch.

## What's missing

**Decor (backlog 12)**
- **A decor catalogue** (content file). Each piece has:
  - id, name key and style
  - cosiness (1–5) and price
  - source: task, coins, event or premium
  - model
- **The save is ready but unused.** `cafe.owned` and `cafe.placed` exist and nothing fills them.

- **Placement:**
  - snap to a 0.25 m grid
  - rotate in 90° steps
  - tap a piece to swap it
  - no overlaps, and floor and wall rules
- **Placement rules in plain C#** (`DecorLayout`: fits, overlaps, snap), with tests.
- **Changing a task's style later for free** (`GAME_SYSTEMS.md` 9; DEVLOG 2026-10-30).
- **Screens:** decor shop, placement, style picker, cosiness panel, task list (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 12).
- **Prices:** each piece's price still needs to go into `balance.json` (02).

**Cosiness**
- The score is the sum of placed pieces (1–5 each), plus a bonus for 3 or more pieces in one style.
- Higher scores add walk-in tables (`levelB.cafe.cosinessForExtraTable`) and more tips. The tip number is missing (02).
- `cafe.cosiness` is saved but never worked out.

**Camera (backlog 11)**
- Cinemachine is installed but unused. Either move to Cinemachine (`BUILD_GUIDE.md` 11), or keep the small script and update the guide.
- Double-tap a table to focus on it (`SYSTEMS_B.md` 2).
- With reduced motion on, camera moves become cuts (`COMPLETE_SPEC.md` 5).

**Time of day and weather**
- The spec has four times of day and the code has three (02). Each needs its own light, music and regulars.
- The time is set once at launch and doesn't change while the app stays open.
- No weather yet: rain and fog in winter, chosen by date. It's only for mood.

**Spaces**
- Only the café floor exists.
- Still to build: the kitchen corner, the rooftop (chapter 6), and Teta's back room (memories only).

**Performance (backlog 11, `PLAN.md` 5)**

- The budget is about 150K triangles, under 150 draw calls and textures up to 2K.
- Use baked lighting, and add a quality setting that lowers shadows and effects (23).
