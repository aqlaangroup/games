Engineering notebook

Any number, opened
WINGWRIGHTNotebook · RangeExport CSV
Range, Breguet equation (propeller aircraft)
R = (ηp / c) · (L / D) · ln(W0 / W1)
ηp = 0.80   propeller efficiency at cruise
c = 0.30 kg/kWh = 8.18 × 10−7 m−1   specific fuel consumption
L/D = 10.0   at cruise CL = 0.42
W0 / W1 = 1,250 / 1,100 kg   start / end of cruise
R = 0.80 / 8.18×10−7 × 10.0 × ln(1.136) = 1,250 km still air
− climb, descent and 45 min reserve = 940 km mission range
Why it matters: a 10% better L/D adds about 125 km. The biggest lever on this aircraft is the strut drag. Click any input to jump to the part that drives it.

What happened

The spar cap yielded at 2.9 g, before the required ultimate load of 5.7 g (3.8 g limit × 1.5).

Location62% span, upper cap
Material7075-T6
Cap thickness18 mm
Stress at failure512 MPa
Yield strength503 MPa
Ways to fix it
Cap to 26 mm at 40–75% span+6.1 kg
Move strut attach outboard 0.4 m+1.2 kg
Switch cap to CFRP−3.4 kg · +$9K

Failure is the best teacher when it's specific. The result says where it failed, at what load and against which limit, then offers real fixes with their weight and cost.
