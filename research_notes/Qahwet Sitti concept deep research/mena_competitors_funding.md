# MENA Competitors and Funding Landscape for "Qahwet Sitti" (merge-2 + story + café makeover, Arab women 25–45)

Research date: 30 Oct 2026. Method: web search (about 16 queries). Note: www.pocketgamer.biz could not be fetched (blocked in this session's network settings), so the PocketGamer.biz "Top 30 MENA Game Makers 2025" list was seen only through search snippets. Most figures below come from search-result summaries of the cited pages, not full-page reads. The report writer should treat single-source numbers as "reported".

## 1. Arabic-made or Arabic-localized casual/story games targeting women: who exists and how they performed

### Takeaway
Arabic-made games aimed at women are rare and small. The main known examples are Tamatem's older localized titles, Fashion Queen and Girls' Secrets (أسرار البنات). Tamatem's own case study called its first women-targeted game "a first" for the company. MENA studios with public numbers (Tamatem, Falafel, Maysalward, Steer, Floaty, Ash Games) mostly make mid-core, RPG/anime, card (Baloot) or trivia games, not female-skewed casual games. I found no public download or revenue figures for any women-targeted Arabic-made casual title.

### Cited Findings
- **Tamatem (Amman, Jordan; founded 2013 by Hussam Hammo):** founded because few Arabic games were available online — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamatem_Games). Its portfolio is reported at over 100M downloads in one source and over 120M in another (TechCrunch, 2021) — [TechCrunch 2021](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/06/tamatemgames-raises-11m-series-b-led-by-pubg-mobile-developer-krafton/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamatem_Games).
- Tamatem's Adjust case study says one of its games was "geared towards women, a first for Tamatem" (undated, probably before 2020) — [Adjust case study](https://www.adjust.com/resources/case-studies/tamatem/).
- Tracxn lists "Fashion queen" and "Girls secrets" in Tamatem's portfolio. A PocketGamer.biz headline describes Fashion Queen as a renamed Hollywood Story partnership, with release date unconfirmed — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/tamatem/__cDVVrAwk-eU2u04OUBaKtQm3QUsVPNMS8vf4D254nxU); [PocketGamer.biz profile](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/industry/tamatem-games/).
- **أسرار البنات اختاري مصيرك (Girls' Secrets: Choose Your Destiny)**, published by Tamatem, is an Arabic interactive-story game with "over 20 Arabian tales" where choices shape the plot. Softonic shows only about 100 downloads last month, a weak signal that it is now small or dormant — [Softonic](https://sr-r-lbn-t-kht-ry-msyrk.en.softonic.com/android).
- Tamatem's focus has moved toward mid-core and anime. Its 2025 launch was Land of Heroes, described as one of the first Arabic anime RPGs. In Dec 2025 it acquired ad-tech firm Playable Factory — [PocketGamer.biz Top 30 MENA 2025 (snippet)](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/the-top-30-mena-game-makers-of-2025/); [PocketGamer.biz profile](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/industry/tamatem-games/).
- **Maysalward (Jordan, founded about 2003 by Nour Khrais):** card and casual titles such as Baloot (Balot) and Arabian Puzzle: Ludo Quest. It is prominent in regional game-dev education — [Google Play: Balot](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.maysalward.balote&hl=en_IN); [The Gaming Nest](https://thegamingnest.com/en/articles/db3940f0-b19b-46db-836a-e4202291d4c8).
- **Falafel Games (Lebanon/UAE; HQ reported inconsistently as Beirut, Abu Dhabi or Dubai):** made Forsan el Majd, described as the first MMO with authentic Arabic content. Its current App Store catalog includes Fictionary and ABJAD – Learn Arabic. It is a CMA Abu Dhabi partner. It has no women-targeted casual titles — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/falafal-games); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/lb/developer/falafel-games-sal/id1171427200); [CMA](https://www.cma.gov.ae/partners-detail/falafel-games).
- **Steer Studios (Saudi, Savvy-owned):** first mobile game Grunt Rush (2025), made by 20 people in about a year. Women are 25% of its staff. It is not a casual or women-targeted game — [The National, Jun 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/2025/06/24/saudi-arabia-steer-studios-grunt-rush-savvy-games/).
- **MBC Game Studio (Saudi-financed):** 1001: Threads of Mizan, a big-budget title built on Arabic lore. It is not casual — [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/1001-threads-of-mizan-is-a-big-budget-arabic-lore-title-coming-from-the-saudi-financed-mbc-game-studio/).
- **Arab indie titles with female leads (not merge):** Najd (Saudi teen heroine, includes a Saudi coffee level) and Afnan: The Desert Girl (platformer) on itch.io — [Najd](https://thurayax.itch.io/najd); [Afnan](https://afnanlab.itch.io/afnan-the-desert-girl/devlog/283757/-). An all-female Saudi fighter game (NA3M, "Saudi Girls Revolution") was reported years ago, status unknown — [News of Bahrain](https://www.newsofbahrain.com/world/2924.html).
- **Audience signal:** a survey of 247 Saudi women gamers (work-in-progress paper) found a strong preference for casual, narrative-rich and mobile games. It also links demand for Arabic localization to a desire for recognition, and reports fear of harassment from male players — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/394251414_The_Surprising_Gaming_Profile_of_Saudi_Women).
- Locally made Arabic games do chart in Saudi Arabia: Tarbi3ah Baloot ranked #28 top-grossing on Google Play KSA as of 26 Oct 2026 — [AppBrain KSA top grossing](https://www.appbrain.com/stats/google-play-rankings/top_grossing/game/sa).

### Inferences
- Female-skewed casual games are an underserved niche among Arab studios. The regional money (Tamatem, Savvy, Merak) has gone to mid-core, RPG, card and esports-adjacent games.
- Tamatem's older women titles (Fashion Queen, Girls' Secrets) show the publisher has tested this audience before, but with localized, story-only formats, not merge.

### Gaps
- No public downloads or revenue for Fashion Queen, Girls' Secrets or any Arabic-made women-targeted title.
- **Snowball, Wizz, Babil Games, Quirkat, Ubisoft Abu Dhabi, Thunderful:** searches returned nothing specific on women-targeted or casual titles or their performance. Babil Games was named only as a market participant. A search for "Wizz" returned unrelated Merge Hotel clones. Background knowledge (unverified in this session): Ubisoft Abu Dhabi made casual mobile games, and Quirkat is an older Jordanian studio. Both need confirmation.
- No Egyptian casual or women's studio surfaced.
- The full PocketGamer.biz Top 30 MENA 2025 list could not be read (site blocked).

## 2. Do the global merge leaders support Arabic, and how do they rank in KSA/UAE/Egypt?

### Takeaway
Gossip Harbor is a top-10 grossing game on Google Play in Saudi Arabia (#9, 26 Oct 2026), and Arabic-language sites describe it as available in Arabic. Travel Town ranks #46. The merge-plus-story format already makes strong money in KSA with non-Arab heroines. I found no data on Merge Mansion, Seaside Escape, the UAE or Egypt.

### Cited Findings
- AppBrain, Google Play Saudi Arabia top-grossing games (updated 26 Oct 2026): **Gossip Harbor #9** (about 98M installs globally) and **Travel Town #46** (about 50M installs) — [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/stats/google-play-rankings/top_grossing/game/sa).
- Similarweb KSA top-grossing snapshots (2026): Gossip Harbor #30 on Google Play (4 Oct), #27 on the iPhone chart (28 Sep) and #19 on the iPad chart (17 Sep) — [Similarweb Google](https://www.similarweb.com/top-apps/google/saudi-arabia/top-grossing/); [Similarweb iPhone](https://www.similarweb.com/top-apps/apple/saudi-arabia/top-grossing/).
- Gossip Harbor overtook Travel Town as the top-grossing merge game around the end of 2024 and kept the lead through H1 2025. It was in the Google Play top-10 grossing games in 28 of 30 countries — [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/how-gossip-harbor-conquered-us-market-in-2025/); [Naavik](https://naavik.co/digest/gossip-harbors-meteoric-rise-in-merge/); [DEV Community](https://dev.to/glitchbound/roblox-royal-match-and-candy-crush-rankings-api-the-top-grossing-mobile-games-in-30-countries-on-3no7).
- An Arabic Android site describes Gossip Harbor as playable in Arabic, with heroine "Quinn" in a coastal town — [myandroid-apps](https://www.myandroid-apps.com/download-gossip-harbor-game/). This is a secondary source. Confirm on the store listing.
- Café and diner merge games already exist with Arabic store listings but non-Arab heroines: Merge Inn ("لعبة مقهى ودمج", heroine Maisie) and Merge Haven (café rebuild, heroine Liza) — [Merge Inn](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mergegame.merge&hl=en_US); [Merge Haven](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cleverapps.garden&hl=en_US).

### Inferences
- A Saudi spender base for merge-plus-story already exists and pays at top-10 level. Qahwet Sitti would compete for the same wallet, with cultural authenticity as its difference.
- Even if a global leader localizes into Arabic, its narrative is Western. No leader offers Khaleeji or Levantine dialect story content (inferred; not verified title by title).

### Gaps
- Arabic language support for Travel Town, Merge Mansion and Seaside Escape is not confirmed. Check the App Store "Languages" field.
- No UAE or Egypt rankings, and no Sensor Tower or AppMagic revenue by country, were found.

## 3. Is there a direct competitor ("Arab heroine + merge + café/home + dialect story")?

### Takeaway
None found. English and Arabic searches ("لعبة دمج عربية مقهى قصة بطلة عربية") turned up café merge games with Western heroines, Arab-heroine indie games that are not merge, and Tamatem's old Arabic choice-story game. Nothing combines all four elements.

### Cited Findings
- Closest adjacent titles: Merge Inn and Merge Haven (café merge, non-Arab heroines); Gossip Harbor (merge + restaurant + story, reportedly in Arabic, non-Arab heroine); Najd (Saudi heroine plus Saudi coffee scene, not merge); Girls' Secrets (Arabic interactive story by Tamatem, not merge) — [Merge Inn](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.mergegame.merge&hl=en_US); [Merge Haven](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.cleverapps.garden&hl=en_US); [Najd](https://thurayax.itch.io/najd); [Softonic](https://sr-r-lbn-t-kht-ry-msyrk.en.softonic.com/android).
- An adult "merge" app (Passion Industry, 18+, .ae domain) appeared in the Arabic search. It is not a fit and is a brand-safety consideration only — [passionindustry.ae](https://passionindustry.ae/).

### Inferences
- The specific combination looks unclaimed, but the risk is a fast follower. Tamatem, or a global merge studio adding an "Arabian café" event, could copy it.

### Gaps
- No app-store keyword scan or AppMagic search was possible. Small or new titles (under 100K downloads) could exist undetected.

## 4. Publishing, funding and support options (with numbers)

### Takeaway
The most realistic options for a small Jordan-linked casual studio are:
- KAFD / Jordan Gaming Lab micro-grants (JD 5K individual / JD 10K startup)
- a Tamatem publishing deal on revenue share
- Saudi seed capital: Merak Gaming Fund ($80M fund; recent rounds of $480K–$1.5M), Impact46, and NEOM Level Up / Savvy Nine66 acceleration
- Media City Qatar's gaming incentive, which requires a Qatar license and local staff

Abu Dhabi's famous 35–50% cashback rebate is for film and TV. I found no evidence that game development qualifies. Dubai DPG33 offers visas and setup support, not published cash grants.

### Cited Findings

**Publishers**
- **Tamatem (Jordan):** publishes third-party games in MENA under revenue-share agreements negotiated per developer. An aggregator also says it earns publishing fees. Exact split percentages are not public — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamatem_Games). It raised an $11M Series B led by Krafton (Dec 2021) at a reported valuation of about $80M. Reported totals raised vary: $17.4M (Tracxn), $22.5M (CB Insights), $23M (PitchBook) — [Forbes ME](https://www.forbesmiddleeast.com/innovation/startups/pubg-creator-krafton-leads-$11m-funding-round-in-jordan-based-arabic-games-publisher-tamatem); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2021/12/06/tamatemgames-raises-11m-series-b-led-by-pubg-mobile-developer-krafton/); [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/tamatem/financials). It acquired Playable Factory (Dec 2025), which adds playable-ad UA capability — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/industry/tamatem-games/).

**Saudi Arabia**
- **Savvy Games Group (PIF-owned, founded 2021):** reported $38B deployment plan. Its Nine66 incubator is linked through an MoU with **NEOM's "Level Up" accelerator** to move startups "from incubation to acceleration". It runs the Savvy Academy university programs (KAU Jul 2025, King Saud Univ. Dec 2025, Alfaisal 2026) — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/saudi-arabia-signs-mou-to-bolster-pipeline-for-video-game-startups); [Savvy](https://www.savvygames.com/news/savvy-games-group-and-king-abdulaziz-university-partner-to-launch-training); [Asharq Al-Awsat](https://english.aawsat.com/business/5179180-savvy-commits-major-investments-boost-gaming-industry-saudi-arabia%C2%A0%C2%A0).
- **National Gaming & Esports Strategy:** a third-party analysis cites a SAR 50B (about $13.3B) GDP target by 2030 — [Lmarks blog](https://lmarks.com/blog/inside-saudi-arabias-38-billion-national-gaming-and-esports-strategy/) (secondary source).
- **Esports Foundation (formerly EWC Foundation, renamed Feb 2026):** a $20M+ stimulus fund in 2025, which goes to esports clubs and is not relevant to casual developers — [SportBusiness](https://www.sportbusiness.com/2025/04/explained-the-strategy-behind-esports-20m-stimulus-fund/); [Esports Advocate](https://esportsadvocate.net/2026/02/saudi-government-changes-the-name-of-esports-world-cup-foundation/).
- **Saudi Game Champions:** a government-backed incubator/accelerator with game-jam entry, partnered with DigiPen (older report) — [Yahoo/LA Times](https://tech.yahoo.com/gaming/articles/fatwas-fortnite-saudi-arabias-38-100000636.html).
- **Merak Capital – Merak Gaming Fund ($80M, MENA games):** Floaty Studio, Riyadh mobile ($1M); Ash Games ($1.5M seed, co-led with Impact46); Majestic Mind Games ($1.45M seed, Merak + Impact46, with NEOM support); Qwacks ($480K pre-seed, 2025); Khosouf Studio ($600K, UAE studio relocating to KSA). Its Exel by Merak accelerator deployed $5.1M+ across 17 startups in cohort 1 — [Jawlah: Floaty](https://jawlah.co/en/60443); [Jawlah: Ash](https://jawlah.co/en/61203); [Jawlah: Majestic Mind](https://jawlah.co/en/62079); [Arab News PK](https://www.arabnews.pk/business/startup-wrap-fresh-capital-flows-into-mena-gaming-fintech-and-ai-startups-2654741); [PocketGamer.biz Merak profile](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/industry/merak-capital/).
- Note: Khosouf's KSA relocation shows a pattern. Saudi money often expects a Saudi presence.

**UAE**
- **Abu Dhabi (CMA / ADFC):** the cashback rebate rose from 30% to **35% base from 1 Jan 2025**, up to **50%** with 85+ points. Caps are $10M (features/HETV), $2M (TV series) and $500K (short form). The 2025 expansion added animation, game shows and other TV formats. **I found no evidence that video-game development qualifies** — [The National, Dec 2024](https://www.thenationalnews.com/arts-culture/film-tv/2024/12/30/abu-dhabi-film-tv-cashback-rebate/); [Lexology](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=dcc07d61-49bc-4b56-8f54-411359ec98c6).
- Gaming support in Abu Dhabi is non-cash in the sources: Yas Creative Hub, a gaming Centre of Excellence, and a twofour54 × Unity gaming hub — [CMA](https://www.cma.gov.ae/news/yas-creative-hub-set-to-triple-jobs-in-abu-dhabi-s-media-entertainment-sector); [Variety 2020](https://variety.com/2020/digital/global/abu-dhabi-yas-creative-hub-media-gaming-entertainment-industry-1234838957/).
- **Dubai Program for Gaming 2033 (Dubai Future Foundation, launched Nov 2023):** targets 30,000 jobs and about $1B in GDP by 2033. Incentives cited are free-zone 100% ownership, simplified registration and a 10-year Golden Visa for gaming talent (via Dubai Culture accreditation; age 25+, portfolio). It has attracted 350+ gaming firms. No published cash grants were found — [Khaleej Times](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/dubai-gaming-industry-booming); [Time Out Dubai](https://www.timeoutdubai.com/news/gaming-golden-visa-dubai).

**Jordan**
- **KAFD (King Abdullah II Fund for Development) via Jordan Gaming Lab:** grants of up to **JD 5,000 (individual) / JD 10,000 (startup)**, applied for through the Lab's fund portal. The second cycle had a 17 July deadline (year unconfirmed). The same caps applied in 2021 — [Jordan Times](https://jordantimes.com/news/local/kafd-launches-second-cycle-funding-support-digital-game-developers); [Jordan News 2021](https://www.jordannews.jo/Section-114/All/KAFD-launches-funding-window-for-game-developers-4520). The Lab hosted Game Jam 2026 with 80 participants across 4 branches — [Arab Game News](https://www.arabgamenews.com/post/jordan-hosts-game-jam-2026-challenge).
- **MoDEE Jordanian E-Gaming & Esports Strategy 2023–2027:** promises "support, incentives and possible privileges" for gaming SMEs, four national hubs (Amman, Irbid, Zarqa, Aqaba) and a University of Jordan center. No cash amounts are stated — [MoDEE strategy PDF](https://www.modee.gov.jo/ebv4.0/root_storage/en/eb_list_page/jordanian_strategy_for_e-gaming_and_e-sports_2023-2027_(english)-unofficial_translation.pdf); [Petra](https://petra.gov.jo/Include/InnerPage.jsp?ID=77731&lang=en&name=en_news).

**Qatar**
- **Media City Qatar Gaming Incentive:** open only to MCQ-licensed entities. The amount is based on qualifying spend under MCQ guidelines. The application asks for a budget by development stage, the number of full-time staff based in Qatar, and confirmation of no gambling content. No published percentage was found — [MCQ application](https://mediacity.qa/en/gaming-incentive-application/). Other activity: SEGA MoU (Oct 2025) — [QNA](https://qna.org.qa/en/News-Area/News/2025-10/22/media-city-qatar-sega-partner-to-develop-video-game-industry); Geekend Game Jam (Nov 2024) top prize of QAR 25,000 — [MCQ](https://mediacity.qa/en/geekend-game-jam-wraps-up-with-prizes-and-opportunities-to-empower-qatars-game-developers/); Invest Qatar at Gamescom 2025 — [Gulf Times](https://www.gulf-times.com/article/709418/qatar/invest-qatar-mcq-showcase-qatars-gaming-ecosystem-at-gamescom-2025-in-germany).

**Other VCs**
- BITKRAFT made 19 investments in 2025. The regional one found was Circle Games (Turkey), not an Arab-world deal — [Mobidictum](https://mobidictum.com/turkish-gaming-companies-that-received-investment/). Wamda's gaming topic page tracks regional deals — [Wamda](https://www.wamda.com/topic/gaming).

### Inferences
- Realistic funding stack for a pre-launch Jordan studio: KAFD grant (about $7K–14K) for a prototype or vertical slice, then a Tamatem or Saudi publisher pitch, or a Merak/Impact46 pre-seed or seed ($0.5–1.5M) once there is a soft-launch KPI deck. NEOM Level Up and Savvy Nine66 are acceleration routes but likely require a Saudi entity.
- Abu Dhabi and Qatar incentives require setting up locally (licensing and local headcount). They are relevant only if the studio relocates or opens a branch.
- A women-targeted casual merge game would stand out in Merak's portfolio, which is mostly core and mid-core, and in Tamatem's current mid-core/anime pipeline. That is a pitch angle, not a confirmed investor appetite.

### Gaps
- **Not found:** Tamatem's revenue-split percentages; any Saudi GEA (General Entertainment Authority) or Ministry of Investment gaming grants; Misk or "Game Lab" accelerator terms; Qiddiya studio funding; Sandooq Al Watan gaming deals; Abu Dhabi Gaming (the 2024 initiative) incentive specifics; MCQ incentive percentage; ICT Ministry cash programs in Jordan; the year of KAFD's second cycle.
- No Wamda or Magnitt aggregate MENA gaming VC totals for 2025–26 were retrieved.
