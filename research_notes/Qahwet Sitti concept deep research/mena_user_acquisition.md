## 1. CPI, CPM, IPM and ROAS benchmarks (KSA, UAE, Egypt, Jordan; iOS vs Android; vs US)

### Takeaway
No public source gives casual- or puzzle-specific CPI by country for KSA, UAE, Egypt or Jordan. The best available data is MENA-wide gaming data from Adjust, covering all game genres. On that data MENA is cheap for gaming UA: 2025 CPM was about $1.49 against $4.34 globally, and MENA had the highest IPM of any region in 2024 (11.56). The UAE is getting expensive fast (CPM $3.25, CPI +54% in 2025), while Saudi CPI rose only about 5%. Egypt and Jordan have no published game-level benchmarks, so treat them as low-cost Android-heavy markets to verify with test campaigns.

### Cited Findings
**MENA / country (Adjust, all game genres) [VENDOR]**
- 2025 data (Adjust Gaming App Insights Report 2026 edition): MENA gaming CPM fell about 3% to **$1.49**. UAE CPM rose about 55% to **$3.25**. No Saudi CPM was reported for 2025 ("n/a"). — [GameDev Reports summary of Adjust 2026](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/adjust-gaming-app-insights-report); figures relayed via [Game Growth Advisor MENA guide 2026](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-28-mena-gaming-market-entry-guide-2026/) (secondary source)
- 2025 data: MENA gaming CPI rose about **15%**. By country: **UAE +54%**, Turkey +43%, **Saudi Arabia +5%**. Saudi game day-1 retention reportedly reached 21%. — [Game Growth Advisor (secondary, citing Adjust)](https://gamegrowthadvisor.com/blog/2026-05-28-mena-gaming-market-entry-guide-2026/). Absolute MENA or Saudi CPI dollar values did not appear in any extract.
- 2025 data: MENA gaming installs rose about 2% and sessions about 7% YoY. — [Adjust press release, Business Wire, Mar 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260310424022/en/Adjust-Gaming-App-Sessions-Rose-In-2025-As-Retention-Becomes-A-Core-Growth-Strategy)
- 2024 data (Adjust 2025 edition): **Saudi CPM rose from $1.82 to $3.45** and **UAE CPM from $1.58 to $2.15**. — [InvestGame, Adjust Gaming App Insights 2025](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/the-gaming-app-insights-report-2025-edition/) / [PDF](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/gamingreport2025_ebook_en.pdf)
- 2024 data: **MENA IPM was 11.56, the highest of any region**. — [GameDev Reports summary of Adjust Mobile Games Insights 2025](https://gamedevreports.substack.com/p/adjust-mobile-games-insights-report)
- Inconsistency: the Saudi CPM figures for 2024 ($3.45) and 2025 (MENA $1.49, Saudi n/a) don't line up cleanly. The 2025 "n/a" may mean Adjust's sample was too small. One search summary also flagged the Adjust 2025 PDF extract for KSA/UAE as garbled. Verify against the PDF.

**Global and US comparison [VENDOR]**
- 2025: global gaming CPI rose about 30% to **$0.56** and global gaming CPM about 20% to **$4.34** (from $3.63). **North America gaming CPI was $1.68** (+31% from $1.28). — [InvestGame summary of Adjust 2026 edition](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/2026-03-26-gaming-app-insights-report-2026_wp/); [PDF](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2026-03-26-gaming-app-insights-report-2026_wp.pdf)
- On these figures, MENA CPM ($1.49) is about one third of the global gaming CPM ($4.34). The UAE ($3.25) is close to global levels.

**Casual genre, global, iOS vs Android (Liftoff, Feb 2024–Feb 2025 data) [VENDOR]**
- Casual games median CPI: **$1.41 on iOS** and **$0.14 on Android**. Several summaries repeat these figures. One search summary flagged the Android number as unusually low (it may reflect a blended or eCPI definition), so verify it. — [Liftoff 2025 Casual Gaming Apps Report](https://liftoff.ai/2025-casual-gaming-apps-report/); [Liftoff highlights blog](https://liftoff.ai/blog/highlights-2025-casual-gaming-apps-report/)
- Casual **D7 ROAS is 7.6%** blended (7.8% on iOS). By subgenre: simulation 8.5%, lifestyle 8.3%, **puzzle 6.9%**. — [Udonis casual games 2026 (summarising Liftoff)](https://www.blog.udonis.co/mobile-marketing/mobile-games/casual-games) [AGENCY/BLOG relay]
- IPM is much higher on Android than iOS for casual games. For example, kids games reach 4.3 IPM on iOS and 6.1 on Android. — [Liftoff 2025 Casual Gaming Apps Report](https://liftoff.ai/2025-casual-gaming-apps-report/)
- 28% of casual game installs from non-gaming publishers came from utility apps. — [Liftoff PR via PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/28-of-casual-game-installs-from-non-gaming-publishers-come-from-utility-apps-liftoffs-casual-gaming-apps-report-reveals-302441607.html)

**Other heuristics [AGENCY/BLOG]**
- One 2026 guide gives a cost multiplier of **0.6–1.0x the Tier-1 English markets** for MENA (UAE, KSA), with wide variance. This is a heuristic, not measured data. — [FoxData 2026 CPI benchmarks](https://foxdata.com/en/blogs/2026-mobile-game-user-acquisition-cost-benchmarks-how-much-should-you-spend/)
- Android game CPI is quoted at $1.50–$4.00 (an older, generic figure, about 20 months old). — [Business of Apps CPI rates](https://www.businessofapps.com/ads/cpi/research/cost-per-install/)
- TikTok CPI for casual games is quoted at $0.80–$2.50, not broken out by MENA. — [Admiral Media TikTok for games](https://admiral.media/tiktok-ads-for-mobile-games/)

### Inferences
- The Gulf is cheap to reach on impressions and converts well (high IPM). But the casual genre's thin D7 ROAS (puzzle about 6.9%) means payback depends on LTV. For a merge + story game, KSA and UAE iOS are likely the value markets. Egypt Android (and probably Jordan) is likely a cheap-volume and ad-monetization (IAA) market where IAP payback will be weak.
- The UAE's rapid CPM and CPI inflation in 2025 suggests starting soft-launch scaling in KSA (stable CPI) and treating the UAE as a smaller, pricier, high-ARPU slice.
- Plausible planning ranges (inference, not sourced): GCC Android casual CPI in the sub-$1 range, GCC iOS CPI near or above the Liftoff casual iOS median of $1.41, and Egypt CPI a fraction of GCC. Women 25–45 targeting will likely cost more than broad targeting. Validate all of these with a $2–5k test per geo.

### Gaps
- No casual- or puzzle-specific CPI, CPM, IPM or ROAS by country for KSA, UAE, Egypt or Jordan, and no iOS-vs-Android split by country in accessible sources.
- No Egypt or Jordan game CPI or CPM from any MMP in the extracts.
- No benchmark for CPI when targeting female audiences in MENA.
- 2026 (YTD) country-level data not found.

## 2. Ad network mix in MENA, and Snapchat's reach among Saudi women

### Takeaway
No public source ranks ad networks for MENA gaming specifically. Global AppsFlyer rankings put Google Ads first on Android, Apple Ads and AppLovin first on iOS, and Mintegral and AppLovin rising. Snapchat is close to universal in KSA (about 89–92% of adults and teens 13+, about 25.3M ad reach in late 2025). Its Saudi ad audience is about 43.5% female, which makes it a core channel for Saudi women. Meta (Instagram) and TikTok round out the social channels.

### Cited Findings
- AppsFlyer Performance Index 2026 (published 28 Oct 2026): covers 57 media sources and 27.2B installs. **Apple Ads and AppLovin lead iOS. Google Ads dominates Android.** Meta leads remarketing. The quality metric changed to QoQ ad-spend change (no longer retention). The index has regional tables (11 regions), but the MENA cut did not appear in any extract. — [AppsFlyer Performance Index 2026](https://www.appsflyer.com/resources/reports/performance-index/) [VENDOR]
- AppsFlyer Performance Index 2025: **Google Ads led Android gaming, while AppLovin, Mintegral and rewarded platforms (e.g. adjoe) made major gains.** It covered 88 media sources and 16.2B non-organic installs. — [AppsFlyer PR 2025](https://www.appsflyer.com/company/newsroom/pr/performance-index-2025/); [adjoe](https://adjoe.io/blog/adjoe-ranks-best-rewarded-ad-network-appsflyer-performance-index-2025/) [VENDOR]
- AppsFlyer 17th Index (2024): Unity Ads was third globally, with strong second places in Match, Shooting, Strategy and Tabletop. Meta was fourth. — [ArabAd](https://www.arabaddigital.com/en/article/5379-Increase-in-App-Install-Ad-Spend-Boosting-Spending-for-Top-Media-Sources-reveals-AppsFlyer-s-17th-Performance-Index)
- **Snapchat KSA reach:** ad reach of about **25.3M in late 2025 (72.9% of total population)**. 91.8% of the 13+ population and **89.0% of adults 18+** use it (DataReportal Digital 2026 KSA, relayed). — [BIMO Snapchat Saudi benchmark 2026](https://www.bimogroup.co/blog/snapchat-ads-saudi-gcc-benchmark); [DataReportal Snapchat stats](https://datareportal.com/essential-snapchat-stats)
- **Snapchat Saudi gender split: 43.5% female, 55.5% male** (adult ad audience, late 2025). — [BIMO](https://www.bimogroup.co/blog/snapchat-ads-saudi-gcc-benchmark) [AGENCY/BLOG relaying DataReportal]
- Snap says it reaches more than 90% of 13–34-year-olds in KSA and that 71% of Saudi parents use Snapchat. — [Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2548521/media) [VENDOR quote]
- Snap opened the "Majlis Snap" creator hub in Riyadh (Dec 2024) to grow local creators. — [AGBI](https://www.agbi.com/analysis/media/2024/12/snap-snapchat-saudi-arabia-office-creator-hub-mena-social-media/)
- TikTok's MENA gaming example: EA Sports FC Mobile's launch led with KSA and used a Branded Mission, an Arabic hashtag and TopView. No CPI was disclosed, and it is a sports title. — [TikTok for Business case](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en-US/inspiration/ea-sports-fc-mobile) [VENDOR]
- TikTok gaming CPI case studies (not MENA): HOMA "Clean It!" reported 2.5M installs, 55% lower CPI and +60% CVR using Creative Challenge, AEO and VBO. Whatwapp's Scopa reported -14% Android CPI with Smart+. — [TikTok gaming performance](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/en/gaming-performance) [VENDOR]
- Agency claims that Ramadan YouTube and Display CPMs can rise 30–60% are unaudited. No official audited public dataset of Saudi Instagram ad costs exists. — [Hikmah AI Instagram KSA benchmarks](https://www.hikmahaiagency.com/blog/instagram-ads-benchmarks-saudi-arabia-2026); [Hovi Google Ads KSA](https://thehovi.com/blog/agency-insights/google-ads-saudi-arabia-complete-ppc-guide-2026) [AGENCY/BLOG]

### Inferences
- A suggested starting mix for women 25–45: **Meta (Instagram/FB, AEO/VO) + Snapchat (KSA-heavy, female-skewed creative) + TikTok (KSA/Egypt, Smart+/Creative Challenge)** for social. **Google App Campaigns (UAC)** for Android scale, especially Egypt and Jordan. Add **AppLovin / Mintegral / Unity** programmatic once there is enough event volume for ROAS bidding, and once IAA is live (playables matter there).
- Snapchat's 43.5% female share, applied to about 25M reach, implies roughly 11M Saudi women reachable (simple arithmetic, not a sourced figure).

## 3. Influencer, creator and UGC marketing for games in KSA/UAE; costs; regulation (Mawthooq, UAE Advertiser Permit)

### Takeaway
Saudi creator rates are published only by agencies and vary 5–10x between sources. Micro creators (10K–100K) cost about SAR 2,500–15,000 per post, and nano creators (1K–10K) about SAR 500–3,000. No public game-install case study from Saudi or UAE female creators was found. Both markets now license paid promotion: Saudi Mawthooq costs SAR 15,000 for 3 years (2022 figure), and the UAE Advertiser Permit was launched in July 2025 and is mandatory from about February 2026. Brands should hire only licensed creators.

### Cited Findings
**Costs [AGENCY/BLOG; vary widely]**
- KSA: nano (1K–10K) about SAR 750–3,000 per post. Micro (10K–100K) about SAR 3,000–15,000. Macro (100K–1M) about SAR 15,000–75,000. — [Influencer Marketing Hub KSA guide 2026](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-in-saudi-arabia-guide/)
- KSA: nano SAR 500–2,500 and micro SAR 2,500–15,000 per post, depending on niche, format and platform. Top-tier Snapchat stories can cost six figures. — [Catchers Agency](https://catchers.agency/blog/influencer-marketing-cost-in-saudi-arabia/); [Deepreach](https://deepreach.sa/how-influencer-marketing-works-saudi-arabia/)
- A focused micro-influencer campaign in KSA is suggested at SAR 30,000–100,000. — [Influencer Marketing Hub KSA](https://influencermarketinghub.com/influencer-marketing-in-saudi-arabia-guide/)
- Global benchmarks: TikTok micro (10K–50K) about $25–125 per post ([Socially Powerful](https://sociallypowerful.com/influencer/marketing/cost)). Snapchat micro (15K–75K) about $1K–5K per post ([Business of Apps 2024](https://www.businessofapps.com/marketplace/influencer-marketing/research/influencer-marketing-costs/)). Snapchat is generally priced above TikTok at the same tier.

**UGC and dialect for ads [AGENCY/BLOG]**
- Saudi viewers can tell instantly whether a creator is Saudi, Emirati, Egyptian or Levantine, and this affects trust. Use Najdi or Hijazi for KSA and Emirati/Khaleeji for the UAE and wider GCC. — [OneHub Arabic UGC creators](https://onehub.ae/arabic-ugc-creators-for-gcc-ads/)

**Saudi regulation: Mawthooq (GCAM / now General Authority for Media Regulation)**
- The license is issued by GCAM, costs **SAR 15,000 and is valid for 3 years** (2022 launch details). It is needed when promotion is a source of income or done commercially. Non-Saudi residents must be represented by approved advertising agencies. — [Arab News 2022](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2140406/amp); [Clyde & Co 2022](https://www.clydeco.com/en/insights/2022/09/new-social-media-requirements-for-saudi-arabia); [Arab News Mawthooq rules](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2176661/media)
- Saudipedia (Oct 2025) names the regulator as the General Authority of Media Regulation. It lists covered platforms as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Snapchat and X. — [Saudipedia](https://saudipedia.com/en/what-is-the-mawthooq-license)
- Penalties include fines or license suspension. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/world/gulf/saudi/saudi-arabia-tightens-rules-on-influencer-ads-with-mandatory-licensing-1.1733131320843). Claims of fines up to SAR 5M and a 35% shrink in the creator pool come from a marketing blog and are UNVERIFIED.

**UAE regulation: Advertiser Permit (UAE Media Council, July 2025)**
- Applies to anyone posting ads (paid or unpaid) on social media, websites or apps, including business accounts and in-house teams. Valid for 1 year. Holders must display the permit number and follow 20 content standards. Visiting creators need a Visiting Advertiser Permit through approved agencies. — [The National, 30 Jul 2025](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2025/07/30/uae-social-media-advertiser-permit/); [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/law-update-articles/uae-introduces-mandatory-advertiser-permit-for-content-creators); [Khaleej Times FAQ](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/new-advertiser-permit-who-needs-faqs)
- Fees are waived for the first 3 years. Mandatory from **1 Feb 2026** (Gulf News) or 31 Jan (Pinsent Masons). Fines go up to **AED 1M**. More than 1,800 permits were issued by Sep 2025. — [Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/uae/new-uae-law-advertiser-permit-now-mandatory-for-influencers-and-creators-for-social-media-1.500427938); [Gulf News fines](https://gulfnews.com/living-in-uae/telephone-internet/what-fines-influencers-and-content-creators-face-under-uae-media-law-1.500418948); [The National Sep 2025](https://thenational-the-national-prod.cdn.arcpublishing.com/news/uae/2025/09/10/uae-issues-more-than-1800-advertiser-permits-for-influencers)

### Inferences
- The cheapest workable model for an indie studio: buy **UGC-style content** from licensed micro or nano female creators (Saudi Najdi/Hijazi, Egyptian), then run it as paid ads (Spark Ads on TikTok, Snap Star collabs, Meta partnership ads). Use these rather than relying on organic posts. This turns SAR 2–5K pieces of content into scalable creative.
- Licensing raises the floor cost and shrinks the eligible creator pool. Budget for agency mediation for non-Saudi creators.

### Gaps
- No public case study of a mobile game campaign with female creators in KSA/UAE that reports CPI or installs.
- The current (2026) Mawthooq fee and any amendments were not confirmed. Check GAMR's website.
- No Egypt or Jordan influencer regulation research was done in this pass, and no Egypt or Jordan creator rate data was found.

## 4. Creative strategies that work with Arab women (story/drama, Ramadan, dialect voiceover, fail ads)

### Takeaway
Hard creative-performance data for Arab women is not public. The evidence that exists points to narrative, emotional and social content: Saudi women gamers say they are motivated by social connection and emotional immersion and prefer casual, narrative-rich mobile games. Ramadan is also the region's peak drama-viewing season. Use dialect-matched human voice talent (Saudi for KSA, Egyptian for pan-Arab reach) and test drama and story hooks against fail and puzzle hooks.

### Cited Findings
- **Survey of Saudi women gamers (N=247, work in progress):** 82% call themselves casual gamers and 9% serious gamers. They are mainly driven by **social connection and emotional immersion**, with a strong preference for **casual, narrative-rich, mobile** games. — [ACM, "The Surprising Gaming Profile of Saudi Women"](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3744736.3749348); [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/394251414_The_Surprising_Gaming_Profile_of_Saudi_Women)
- MDPI Behavioral Sciences (2026, N=397 Saudis): 53% of women vs 22% of men call themselves casual gamers. 54.7% play mainly on smartphone or tablet. — [MDPI](https://doi.org/10.3390/bs16020202)
- Women make up **over 37% of the MENA-3 (KSA, UAE, Egypt) player base, up from 32.6% in 2023** (Niko Partners). Google (2021–22): women were more than a third of MENA gamers, and about **40% of them were over 35**. — [Niko Partners](https://nikopartners.com/the-future-of-gaming-in-mena-3-understanding-the-opportunity/); [Think with Google](https://www.thinkwithgoogle.com/intl/en-emea/consumer-insights/consumer-trends/win-ramadan-mena-with-2-gaming-audiences/)
- Campaign ME: about 48% of active Saudi gamers are women, and 61% of Saudi female gamers prefer smartphones (Gaming Analytics survey, date and sample not given). — [Campaign Middle East](https://campaignme.com/test-and-learn-approach-to-saudi-female-gamer-boom/). An older AdColony study found 90% of surveyed Saudi women called themselves mobile gamers, against 83% of men. — [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/highlights-from-research-on-mobile-gaming-in-the-middle-east/) (older, pre-2022)
- Niko Partners: accurate text translation is the 3rd most important localization factor for players. Japanese titles launched with Arabic localization saw a 33.2% uplift in GCC sales share. Localization covers Ramadan/Eid events, Arabic VO and RTL. — [Niko Partners](https://nikopartners.com/the-future-of-gaming-in-mena-3-understanding-the-opportunity/) [VENDOR research firm]
- **Dialect guidance [AGENCY/BLOG]:** name the specific Saudi variety in the brief, since generic Gulf sounds foreign in Riyadh. AI-generated Emirati and Saudi voices are still inconsistent, so human VO is safer. Egyptian has the widest pan-Arab familiarity and suits mass-reach and comedy creative. Arabic on-screen text runs 15–25% longer than English. — [SL-Media Dubai](https://slmedia.ae/blog/arabic-voiceover-brand-videos-dubai/); [OneHub](https://onehub.ae/arabic-ugc-creators-for-gcc-ads/)
- Use MSA for UI and system text, and dialect for character voice, marketing and community. — [Ash Games Studio](https://ashgamesstudio.com/pages/localization.html) [AGENCY]
- Ramadan is the Arab world's biggest TV-drama season ("a month-long Super Bowl"). — [Wikipedia: Arab television drama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_television_drama)
- Google Ramadan guidance: women gamers come together during Ramadan to connect and learn from each other. Ramadan-themed gaming content (e.g. Minecraft Ramadan uploads) more than doubled in 2021 vs 2020. — [Arab News / Google](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2056846/media) (2021–22)
- Snapchat Ramadan cases (non-game): Sephora KSA's AR mini-game targeted Muslim women 18–35 and delivered 2.39x ROI, 37s average lens playtime, and coupons that made up 5% of Ramadan web transactions. Faces KSA video reached Snapchatters 25–34 with +10pt brand awareness. Seera (Arabic short-drama streaming app) ran a Snapchat Ramadan install campaign, but figures were not in the extract. — [Digital Training Academy (Sephora)](http://www.digitaltrainingacademy.com/casestudies/2020/02/snapchat_case_study_sephora_gamifies_loyalty_to_boost_ramadan_sales.php) (2020); [Campaign ME (Faces)](https://campaignme.com/case-study-faces-ramadan-brand-formance-campaign-bridges-offline-and-online-and-delivers-10-pts-lift-on-e-commerce-purchases/); [Snap Seera story](https://forbusiness.snapchat.com/success-stories/seera) [VENDOR]
- Research on Saudi women's nighttime digital behavior during Ramadan exists (case study). The findings were not available in the extract. — [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/399432733_Understanding_Nighttime_Digital_Behavior_During_Ramadan_A_Case_Study_on_Saudi_Women)

### Inferences
- The concept (grandma's coffee house, family story) fits the stated motivations: emotional immersion and social connection. Ad hooks to test: (a) **drama cliffhangers** in Ramadan-musalsal style ("Sitti's secret recipe…"), (b) **merge satisfaction / "fail" ads** (wrong merge, messy majlis), (c) **creator UGC** ("my mom's coffee pot is in this game") in Najdi and Egyptian dialects, (d) **Snapchat AR lens / playable** (the Sephora precedent shows women 18–35 engage with AR mini-games).
- Keep Arabic text short (it runs 15–25% longer than English) and record VO with humans per dialect. Saudi and Emirati AI voices were reported as unreliable.

### Gaps
- No public A/B data on dialect vs MSA, story vs fail ads, or female-voiced creative for games in MENA.
- No Ramadan game-ad case study with install metrics.

## 5. Ramadan and Eid effects on installs, sessions, CPI and spending

### Takeaway
Ramadan raises app usage overall (sessions about +15% YoY in the Middle East, 2025) and shifts it to post-Iftar nights. Gaming's lift differs by vendor: AppsFlyer saw gaming flat in Ramadan 2025, while Adjust saw game installs up strongly in Ramadan and Eid, with KSA leading install growth. Ad costs (CPM) rise in Ramadan as brand budgets flood the auction (agency claims of +30–60%, unaudited). The best plan is pre-Ramadan acquisition plus in-Ramadan live events and monetization.

### Cited Findings
- **AppsFlyer, Ramadan 2025 (UAE, KSA, Qatar, Egypt, Jordan; 800+ apps, 220M installs):** Middle East app revenue passed **$1.7B, +18.6% YoY**. Sessions across all categories rose about 15% YoY. **Gaming was flat: sessions +0.05%, IAP monetization stable (3.26%), non-organic installs -1% YoY.** — [AppsFlyer PR](https://www.appsflyer.com/company/newsroom/pr/ramadan-app-revenue/); [Campaign ME](https://campaignme.com/research-confirms-ramadan-effect-as-regions-mobile-app-revenues-surpass-us1-7-bn/); [Arabian Business](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/business/technology/ramadan-effect-fuels-middle-east-mobile-app-revenues-to-cross-1-7bn-report); [Communicate Online](https://communicateonline.me/news/appsflyer-research-confirms-ramadan-effect-as-middle-east-mobile-app-revenues-surpass-us17-billion-showing-186-yoy-growth/) [VENDOR]
- **Adjust, Ramadan 2024 data (published as the 2025 guide):** game installs in METAP rose 5% (global 0.1%), with **UAE +17% and KSA +11%**. Game sessions rose 3% in METAP, **UAE +12%**. Gaming benefits from nighttime activity after Iftar. — [Adjust Ramadan trends 2025](https://www.adjust.com/blog/ramadan-trends-2025); [Campaign Post](https://www.campaignpost.net/article/key-mobile-app-trends-and-insights-for-ramadan-2025-adjust) [VENDOR]
- **Adjust Ramadan 2026 report (covering Ramadan 2025 data):** **game installs +24% during Ramadan and +27% around Eid** (regional, not KSA-only). Highest overall install growth: **KSA +34%** (all apps). Highest overall session increases: **KSA +15%, Egypt +12%, UAE +9%**. — [InvestGame: Ramadan 2026 app trends (Adjust)](https://investgame.net/news/pdf/2026-01-27-ramadan-app-trends-report/); [PDF](https://investgame.net/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/2026-01-27-ramadan-app-trends-report.pdf) [VENDOR]
- **Conflict:** AppsFlyer (gaming non-organic installs -1%, Ramadan 2025) vs Adjust (game installs +24%, Ramadan 2025). These likely reflect different baselines (YoY vs pre-Ramadan period), samples and organic/paid definitions. Neither has been verified at source.
- Older survey (AdColony/GWI, pre-2022): 48% of Saudi respondents played mobile games 30 minutes to 2 hours daily during Ramadan. — [Think with Google / Arab News](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2056846/media)
- Ad costs: agency claim that YouTube and Display CPMs spike **30–60%** in Ramadan. Unaudited. — [Hovi Google Ads KSA 2026](https://thehovi.com/blog/agency-insights/google-ads-saudi-arabia-complete-ppc-guide-2026) [AGENCY/BLOG]
- Eid: no Eid IAP spike data was found for games. Adjust reports the Eid installs figure above.

### Inferences
- The tactic is to scale UA in the 2–4 weeks before Ramadan, when CPMs are normal. Then run a Ramadan narrative event (Sitti's Ramadan majlis, qatayef/coffee recipes, nightly unlocks timed after Iftar and Suhoor). Use Eid for gifting and bundle sales (Eidiya-style offers). Expect higher CPMs during Ramadan itself. Lean on retargeting and lapsed-user re-engagement then.

## 6. Organic channels: store featuring, Arabic ASO, community (WhatsApp, Instagram)

### Takeaway
Arabic-language supply is thin: various sources say under 1% of store games are in Arabic. Apple runs a "Play in Arabic" subcategory. A fully Arabic listing, with custom product pages and custom store listings that serve Arabic creative to Arabic traffic, is the main organic lever. No game-specific uplift data is public. No evidence was found on WhatsApp or Instagram community tactics for games.

### Cited Findings
- **Less than 1% of games on the App Store and Google Play are in Arabic**, for a region of 60M+ users (agency claim, repeated across sources). — [Tarjama](https://tarjama.com/how-to-localize-your-game-to-conquer-the-mena-market/); [Salman Gul ASO MENA](https://salmangul.com/aso-app-store-optimization-mena-arabic/) [AGENCY/BLOG]
- Use App Store **Custom Product Pages** and Google Play **custom store listings** to serve Arabic-first creative to Arabic traffic and English to expats. This is said to lift conversion more than keyword changes. — [Salman Gul](https://salmangul.com/aso-app-store-optimization-mena-arabic/) [AGENCY/BLOG]
- Native (not machine-translated) Arabic is essential. Ratings and reviews are the strongest conversion factor in MENA. — [GAIT ASO MENA](https://gaitco.com/blog/app-store-optimization-mena) [AGENCY/BLOG]
- Apple's App Store has a **"Play in Arabic"** subcategory, studied academically as a localization showcase that may draw Arabic speakers who don't know English. — [Taylor & Francis, Cogent Arts & Humanities 2024](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/23311983.2024.2433314)
- Google Play (2017): the top-grossing KSA apps and games localize listings and product. Skyscanner's rating rose from 4.62 to 4.77 after Arabic localization (an app, 2017). — [Android Developers Blog](https://android-developers.googleblog.com/2017/05/why-you-should-localize-your-app-or.html)
- Saudi top charts skew to racing, drifting and car games (agency observation). — [Entrepreneur MENA](https://mena.entrepreneur.com/marketing/the-how-to-marketing-your-mobile-game-to-the-mena-region/276691)
- Market spend context: KSA makes up about **59% of MENA-3 games spending**, and the UAE has the highest spend per player (secondary source relaying Niko Partners). — [Virtwave MENA gaming stats 2026](https://www.virtwave.com/blog/middle-east-gaming-statistics-2026) [AGENCY/BLOG relay]

### Inferences
- An Arabic-first, culturally specific game (Saudi/Levantine grandma, coffee and dallah, majlis) is well placed for editorial pitches: Apple's "Play in Arabic", Ramadan and Eid collections, and Google Play's regional editorial. Pitch featuring requests 6–8 weeks ahead of Ramadan.
- Community: given Snapchat's and Instagram's dominance among Saudi women, a likely low-cost path is Instagram and Snapchat channels with recipe and story content, plus WhatsApp Channels for event drops. This is inference only. No evidence on game community performance was found.
